============================================================================================================================================
ATHLETE PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD - DATA DICTIONARY
============================================================================================================================================
Date: 2026-06-12
Scope: Power BI supporting views supplied as SQL definitions.
Database platform: PostgreSQL syntax.

TYPE CONVENTION
- Logical types are documentation-level types, not guaranteed physical PostgreSQL return types.
- DECIMAL covers numeric averages, sums, maxima, ratios, scores, and measurements.
- INTEGER covers counts and whole-number calendar attributes.
- Source unit means the SQL does not perform a unit conversion; confirm the configured unit in the source platform.

COMMON MODELLING RULES
- athlete_key is usually normalized with LOWER(TRIM(COALESCE(...))).
- Daily fact views generally use athlete_key + calendar_date as their business key.
- NULL means the metric was unavailable or could not be calculated; it should not automatically be treated as zero.
- primary_load_index is an internal BI comparison index, not a validated physical workload unit.
- Risk categories are decision-support indicators and should not be treated as medical diagnoses.

1. VIEW: public.bi_dim_athlete
============================================================================================================================================
Purpose: Conformed athlete dimension used to provide one preferred athlete record across Catapult, GymAware, and WHOOP.
Grain: One row per normalized athlete_key.
Business Key: athlete_key
Source View/Table(s): silver_catapult_session; silver_gymaware_athletes; silver_whoop_cycle; silver_whoop_recovery; silver_whoop_sleep; silver_whoop_workout
Notes:
- athlete_key is generated by applying LOWER and TRIM to the first available identifier.
- When the same athlete_key appears in multiple systems, the selected record follows this priority: Catapult, GymAware, WHOOP cycle, WHOOP recovery, WHOOP sleep, WHOOP workout.
- Name-based matching can merge different people who share the same name. A governed cross-system athlete ID is preferable for production use.

Field Name | Logical Type | Unit | Definition | Calculation / Rule
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
athlete_key | TEXT | N/A | Normalized business key used to join athlete records across BI views. | LOWER(TRIM(COALESCE(available athlete identifier fields)))
athlete_name | TEXT | N/A | Preferred athlete name or fallback identifier for display. | First non-null display name or source identifier.
athlete_display_name | TEXT | N/A | Display name supplied by the selected source record. | Direct/derived as noted
athlete_internal_key | TEXT | N/A | Internal athlete identifier supplied by a source system. | Direct/derived as noted
athlete_jersey | TEXT | N/A | Athlete jersey number or jersey identifier, when available. | Direct/derived as noted
team_name | TEXT | N/A | Team or sport value associated with the selected athlete record. | Direct/derived as noted
position | TEXT | N/A | Athlete playing position, currently populated from GymAware when available. | Direct/derived as noted
source_system | TEXT | N/A | Source system from which the preferred athlete dimension record was selected. | Direct/derived as noted

2. VIEW: public.bi_dim_date
============================================================================================================================================
Purpose: Continuous calendar dimension covering the minimum to maximum dates found in all supported source tables.
Grain: One row per calendar date.
Business Key: calendar_date
Source View/Table(s): All Catapult, GymAware, and WHOOP silver tables listed in the SQL definition.
Notes:
- The date range is generated only when at least one source date exists.
- week_start_date follows PostgreSQL DATE_TRUNC('week'), which starts the week on Monday.
- Use week_start_date rather than combining calendar year and week_number near year boundaries.

Field Name | Logical Type | Unit | Definition | Calculation / Rule
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
calendar_date | DATE | Date | Calendar date. | Direct/derived as noted
year | INTEGER | Year | Calendar year number. | EXTRACT(YEAR FROM calendar_date)
quarter | INTEGER | Quarter | Calendar quarter number from 1 to 4. | EXTRACT(QUARTER FROM calendar_date)
month_number | INTEGER | Month | Calendar month number from 1 to 12. | EXTRACT(MONTH FROM calendar_date)
month_name | TEXT | N/A | Three-letter month abbreviation. | TO_CHAR(calendar_date, 'Mon')
week_start_date | DATE | Date | Monday date representing the start of the week. | DATE_TRUNC('week', calendar_date)
week_number | INTEGER | Week | Week number returned by PostgreSQL EXTRACT(WEEK). | Direct/derived as noted
day_of_week_number | INTEGER | Day | ISO day number: Monday=1 through Sunday=7. | EXTRACT(ISODOW FROM calendar_date)
day_name | TEXT | N/A | Three-letter day abbreviation. | TO_CHAR(calendar_date, 'Dy')
is_weekend | BOOLEAN | N/A | TRUE for Saturday or Sunday; otherwise FALSE. | Direct/derived as noted

============================================================================================================================================
RISK SCORING RULES - public.bi_fact_athlete_daily_comparison
============================================================================================================================================
Recovery score < 40 | +3
Recovery score >= 40 and < 67 | +1
Sleep efficiency < 70 | +2
Sleep efficiency >= 70 and < 85 | +1
WHOOP average cycle strain >= 14 | +1
Daily load-index change ratio >= 0.25 | +2
Daily load-index change ratio >= 0.10 and < 0.25 | +1
ACWR load index >= 1.50 | +2
ACWR load index >= 1.20 and < 1.50 | +1
Catapult high-jump count >= 20 | +1
High-intensity zone time >= 0.75 hours | +1

Risk level | High Risk when score >= 6; Medium Risk when score >= 3; otherwise Low Risk.
Recommended action | High Risk -> Reduce load / review athlete; Medium Risk -> Monitor next session; Low Risk -> Normal.

============================================================================================================================================
DATA QUALITY AND INTERPRETATION NOTES
============================================================================================================================================
- Cross-system athlete matching depends on coalesced identifiers and may fall back to names or jersey values.
- Window functions operate on available rows. Missing dates can make 'previous day' or rolling-day labels differ from calendar-day intervals.
- The field load_index_28d_avg is calculated from rows 7 through 27 before the current row, which is a 21-row baseline window.
- SUM returns NULL when all source values are NULL in PostgreSQL; dashboard users should distinguish missing data from zero activity.
- Metric thresholds are embedded business rules and should be reviewed with coaching, sports-science, and medical stakeholders before production use.
- Catapult and GymAware source units should be confirmed against each platform's export configuration.
